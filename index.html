Staffer category surface

The product is seven questions a staffer can act on

The 498 taxa are not a browsing menu. They combine into an evidence-backed oversight finding with an available congressional lever.

Strategy-budget mismatchLow-cost drones underfunded; deterrence rhetoric unsupported by munitions depth.
AffordabilityUnbudgeted delay costs; personnel growth outruns the topline.
Execution riskSchedule slippage; reprogramming churn; current-year plan no longer executable.
Industrial baseSkilled-workforce shortages; supplier dependency; acquisition capacity.
ProprietyThin or contradictory justification; unresolved legal, ethics or audit exposure.
Congressional leversAdd, cut or reallocate; fence funds; require certification, reports or audit.
Evidence standardEvery claim links to request text, tables, audit, estimate or external evidence.
Absence is a findingMissing strategy link, unfunded risk or unaddressed constituency becomes visible.
